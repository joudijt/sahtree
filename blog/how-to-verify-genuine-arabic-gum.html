How to Verify Your Arabic Gum Is Genuine (Not Counterfeit)

July 16, 2026 · 6 min read · By Sihatree

Checking your pack the moment it arrives is the easiest way to catch a problem early.

Genuine Arabic gum should arrive in sealed, undamaged packaging with a visible Halal certification number, a batch code, and clear contact details — bought through an official, verified channel. Here's a practical checklist, why counterfeit stock is a real risk in Malaysia, and what to do if something looks off.

Why counterfeit or unofficial stock is a real risk

Arabic gum's popularity means unofficial resellers sometimes sell repackaged, adulterated, or simply mislabelled products, often at a lower price with no verifiable supply chain. Buying from an unverified source means there's no guarantee of what's actually in the pack, or whether the Halal claim is real.

Signs of a genuine pack

Sealed packaging

No signs of tampering, resealing, or damage to the original seal.

Halal logo + cert number

A visible logo paired with an actual certification number you can check.

Batch or lot code

Traceable to a specific production run, not just a generic label.

Official store purchase

Bought directly through the verified official retailer, not a reseller.

Consistent texture & colour

Matches what's expected for that flavour, batch to batch.

Contact info on pack

A real, reachable contact — not a blank or missing label.

What to do if you suspect a counterfeit

Don't self-diagnose

Stop using the product, keep the packaging and proof of purchase, and contact the seller or official supplier directly. Don't continue using a product you suspect may be counterfeit or adulterated.

The safest verification step isn't inspecting the pack after the fact — it's buying from a verified source in the first place.
