# Agent actions — Sihatree

What an automated agent can do on this site, and where.

## Buy retail
- URL: https://sihatree.com/retail
- Method: Redirect to an external marketplace listing
- Notes: The retail page opens a choice of TikTok Shop or Shopee (Arabian Village Malaysia). Checkout, payment and tracking happen inside those platforms — there is no on-site cart or checkout.

## Request wholesale pricing
- URL: https://sihatree.com/wholesale
- Method: On-page enquiry form
- Inputs: name, business name, email, phone, message
- Notes: Bulk and distributor enquiries. Terms are quoted by the seller — no wholesale prices are published.

## Ask before buying
- URL: https://wa.me/601111119912
- Method: WhatsApp message
- Notes: Stock checks, branch opening hours before travelling, and bulk orders. Opening hours are not published anywhere online.

## Read the full machine-readable brief
- URL: https://sihatree.com/llms.txt
- Method: GET
- Notes: English at /llms.txt, Bahasa Melayu at /ms/llms.txt, Arabic at /ar/llms.txt. Each is written natively in that language, not translated.
