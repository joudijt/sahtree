Five Flavours, One Simple Daily Habit

Sihatree Arabic Gum Powder comes in five variants — each Halal-friendly, each built around the same naturally sourced fibre, each easy to mix into whatever you're already drinking.

Shop Retail Wholesale Inquiry

Every Sihatree pack starts from the same base ingredient — Arabic Gum, the naturally hardened sap of the Acacia tree. What changes from one variant to the next is simply what's layered on top of it: nothing, in the case of Original, or a light natural fruit extract for the other four. The idea behind offering five flavours instead of one is straightforward — a soluble-fibre habit only sticks if you actually enjoy taking it, and not everyone wants their water to taste the same way every day.

Some people prefer Original precisely because it disappears into whatever they're drinking, letting them keep their usual glass of water, juice, or coffee exactly as it is while adding the fibre on the side. Others find a plain powder harder to stick with over time, and do better with a flavour they look forward to. Neither approach is more "correct" — the range exists so the choice is yours, not ours.

Flavour 01

Original

The purest expression of Arabic Gum Powder — no fruit extract, no distraction. Original is designed for anyone who wants the fibre without changing the taste of their drink, which is why it suits daily use in plain water or an existing smoothie recipe.

It's also an easy place to start, simply because it removes any guesswork — if you already have a routine you like (a morning glass of water, a particular smoothie, your usual coffee), Original slots into it without changing anything else about your day. That's the whole point of it: one less variable to think about.

Berry Blend

A light, fruity berry note layered over the same Arabic Gum base — built for anyone who prefers a hint of flavour in their daily glass of water, without it tasting like a sugary drink. Often mixed into plain water or a morning smoothie.

Berry Blend tends to be the middle-ground choice — noticeable enough that plain water stops feeling like a chore, but subtle enough that it doesn't compete with breakfast or overwhelm a smoothie that already has its own flavours going on. It's a natural next step for anyone starting out with Original.

Mango

Sweet and tropical, Mango is often chosen by first-time buyers who want a more noticeable flavour. Works well cold, mixed into iced water or juice, especially in warmer weather.

Because the fruit note is stronger here than in Berry Blend, Mango is a good option if a previous fibre supplement put you off by tasting overly "medicinal" or plain — it reads more like a fruit drink than a functional powder, which is exactly why it's a common pick for anyone new to Arabic Gum entirely.

Pineapple

Bright and slightly tangy, Pineapple is a refreshing pick for anyone who finds Original too plain but wants something lighter than Mango. Enjoyed most often chilled, or blended into a fruit smoothie.

The tang sets it apart from the other three fruit variants — where Berry Blend and Mango lean sweet, Pineapple has more of an edge to it, which is why it tends to be the flavour people settle on once they've already tried the sweeter options and want something a little sharper for an afternoon glass.

Pomegranate

The richest and most distinctive flavour in the range. Pomegranate suits those who want a more grown-up, less-sweet profile — a favourite for evening use, mixed into water after dinner as part of a wind-down routine.

It's the flavour with the most personality, and the one people tend to feel strongest about either way — if you've enjoyed pomegranate in other drinks or dishes before, it's usually an easy favourite. Quality & Sourcing

What's Inside Every Pack

Every Sihatree pack shares the same foundation: purified Arabic Gum, milled into a fine, instant powder that dissolves rather than clumps. Original is that ingredient on its own. The four fruit variants add a natural fruit extract to that same base — the fibre content and the way it's sourced don't change from flavour to flavour, only the taste layered on top of it.

Every flavour is naturally suitable for Halal consumers, and is packed in a 150g resealable pouch sized for regular use at home rather than a single serving — enough to build a daily habit around without running out every few days.

Getting Started

How to Use Sihatree

Enjoy

Drink right away, or use in baking and cooking recipes that call for a soluble fibre ingredient.
