Shop Sihatree Online

Order any of our five Halal-friendly flavours for personal use — delivered nationwide across Malaysia through TikTok Shop or Shopee.

Sihatree is sold exclusively through two dedicated online stores — TikTok Shop and Shopee — rather than a standalone checkout on this site. That's a deliberate choice, not a limitation: both platforms already handle payment, delivery tracking, and buyer protection for millions of shoppers across Malaysia, so ordering Sihatree works the same way you'd order anything else you already buy online.

Whichever store you already use day to day is the right one to pick — the product, pricing, and flavour range are the same across both. If you're new to either platform, the store buttons below take you straight to the Sihatree storefront, not a search results page.

If you're ordering for the first time, it's worth checking the flavour picker further down this page before you check out — it links through to a full description of each variant, so you're not guessing between Original and the fruit options based on the product photo alone.

Buying With Confidence

Why Shop Through Our Stores

Ordering through our TikTok Shop and Shopee storefronts — rather than a third-party reseller — means you're getting current stock, the correct pricing, and packaging that hasn't sat unsold for an unknown length of time. It also means any order or delivery issue is handled through the marketplace's own buyer protection, on top of our own support if anything needs following up.

Every flavour listed is the same product described on our Products page — same ingredient, naturally suitable for Halal consumers, same 150g pack size, same ingredient base. There's no separate "retail-only" formulation or downgraded version; what you see here is exactly what ships.

If you're unsure which flavour to start with, our Products page covers each one in detail, or our Benefits page explains what Arabic Gum actually does before you commit to a pack.

After You Order

What Happens Next

Order Confirmation

Your chosen platform (TikTok Shop or Shopee) confirms the order and payment directly — Sihatree doesn't process payments separately, so your usual payment methods on that platform apply as normal.

Dispatch & Tracking

Orders are prepared and dispatched for delivery across Malaysia, with tracking handled through the platform you ordered from — the same tracking experience you'd get from any other order there.
