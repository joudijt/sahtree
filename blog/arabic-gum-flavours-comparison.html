Arabic Gum Flavours Compared: Which One Should You Choose?

July 20, 2026 · 6 min read · By Sihatree

Five flavours, one fibre — the difference comes down to taste, not certification.

All five Sihatree Arabic Gum flavours — Original, Berry Blend, Mango, Pineapple, and Pomegranate — share the same JAKIM Halal certification and fibre content. The real difference is taste, texture in a drink, and which occasion each one suits best. Here's a full side-by-side comparison to help you choose.

Original: the mildest, most versatile choice

Taste: No added flavour — the ingredient in its purest form. Best paired with: Warm water, tea, or any drink where you don't want the Arabic Gum itself to change the taste. Suits: Buyers who want to taste their existing drink unchanged, or who plan to mix it into recipes and cooking rather than drinking it alone.

Berry Blend: fruity and refreshing

Taste: A fruity, slightly tart profile. Best paired with: Cold or iced water, especially in warm weather. Suits: Buyers who find plain Arabic Gum too neutral and want something refreshing to look forward to daily.

Mango: sweet and tropical

Taste: Sweet, tropical, and generally well liked by first-time buyers and kids old enough to have it under guidance. Best paired with: Cold water or diluted into a light drink. Suits: Households introducing Arabic Gum for the first time, where a familiar, sweet taste helps it go down easily.

Pineapple: bright and tangy

Taste: Tangy with a bright, slightly sharp edge. Best paired with: Plain or soda water, where the tang comes through clearly. Suits: Buyers who prefer a more assertive flavour over something sweet or mild.

Pomegranate: richer and more distinctive

Taste: Deeper and more complex than the other fruit flavours. Best paired with: Both hot and cold drinks — it holds up well either way. Suits: Repeat buyers who've already tried the milder options and want something more distinctive as a daily habit.
