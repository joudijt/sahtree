A Natural Fibre Habit, Made Simple

Arabic Gum is a naturally sourced soluble fibre, valued for its gentle prebiotic-style support for gut health. Sihatree turns it into an easy daily ritual — no prep, no complicated routine, just a scoop mixed into whatever you're already drinking.

Most fibre habits fail for the same reason: they ask too much of the day. A capsule you have to remember, a powder that clumps or tastes chalky, a routine that only survives the first week. Arabic Gum sidesteps most of that by being genuinely simple — one ingredient, one scoop, no extra steps — which is really the whole story behind why Sihatree exists as a product in the first place.

The four things below aren't marketing claims stacked on top of each other. They're the same idea looked at from four angles: where the fibre comes from, what it does once it's in your system, how little effort it takes to use, and how far it stretches beyond a single glass of water.

Naturally Sourced

Straight From the Acacia Tree

Arabic Gum is the naturally hardened sap of the Acacia tree — nothing synthetic, nothing manufactured from scratch in a lab. Sihatree simply purifies and mills that raw sap into a fine, instant powder, keeping the ingredient as close to its natural form as possible.

This matters more than it might sound — a lot of fibre supplements on the market are isolated or chemically modified starches. Arabic Gum, by contrast, has been used in food and traditional preparations for generations, long before it was ever sold as a "supplement" — Sihatree isn't introducing a new ingredient, just making an old one easier to use daily.

Gut Health

Prebiotic-Style Fibre Support

Arabic Gum is a soluble fibre widely recognised for its prebiotic-style properties — it can act as a food source for beneficial bacteria already living in your gut. It isn't a probiotic itself (that would mean live bacteria), but a fibre that supports a healthy gut environment as part of a balanced diet.

It's worth being precise about the distinction, since the two terms get mixed up often: a probiotic adds live organisms to your gut, while a prebiotic — which is what Arabic Gum is — feeds the organisms already there. Both can be useful, but they're solving different problems, and Sihatree is specifically the second kind.

Convenience

Built for a Real Daily Routine

The biggest barrier to any wellness habit is friction — so Sihatree is designed to have none. One scoop, mixed into a glass of water, juice, or your usual smoothie, dissolves without extra steps. No soaking, no cooking, no special equipment required.

This is deliberate, not incidental. A habit that requires you to change your routine — a new drink to buy, a new time of day to remember, a new step to add — tends to fall away within a few weeks. Sihatree is built to attach to something you're already doing, which is a much better predictor of whether a habit actually lasts.

Building the Habit

Who This Is For

Sihatree tends to suit people already looking for a small, sustainable change rather than a dramatic one — someone who wants to add more fibre into an otherwise normal diet without overhauling it, or who has tried other fibre supplements before and found them unpleasant to take consistently. It's also a common starting point for people newly exploring gut-health habits who want something low-effort to begin with, rather than a complicated multi-step regimen.

It isn't a replacement for a balanced diet, and it isn't marketed as a treatment for any condition — it's a daily-use fibre ingredient, and the honest way to think about it is as one small, consistent input rather than a single fix. Anyone with an existing health condition, or who is pregnant or breastfeeding, should check with a doctor before adding any new fibre supplement to their routine.
