Berkat Madinah Store's Growth: 6,000+ Products, One Name

July 20, 2026 · 5 min read · By Sihatree

From a single shop to a name serving hundreds of thousands daily — growth built one branch at a time.

Berkat Madinah Store started as one small shop in Ampang in 2010 and now operates 5 branches, stocks 6,000+ products, and reports reaching 500,000+ customers a day. Here's that growth laid out as a timeline, plus where the business says it's headed next.

Where did it start?

2010: a single shop opens in Ampang, Selangor, serving the local Arab and Middle Eastern community with Halal Arab food. At that point, it's just retail — no wholesale line, no cafés, no additional branches.

How did it expand from one shop to five branches?

Over the years that followed, the business grew into three distinct sectors — wholesale distribution, retail branches, and restaurants and cafés serving Syrian and Yemeni cuisine. By 2026, that expansion had produced 5 branches, all within the Klang Valley: two in Ampang (including a dedicated Cash & Carry outlet), plus Kajang, Shah Alam, and Gombak in Kuala Lumpur.

What's next for Berkat Madinah Store?

The company has stated a goal of reaching 6 major branches in Malaysia by 2030 — a measured next step up from its current 5, rather than a sudden nationwide rollout. That pace matches the rest of its growth so far: steady expansion sector by sector, not a rush to open everywhere at once.
