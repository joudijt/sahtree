Where to Buy Arabic Gum Powder in Malaysia (2026 Guide)

July 11, 2026 · 6 min read · By Sihatree

Sihatree Original Arabic Gum Powder — available retail and wholesale across Malaysia.

Arabic Gum powder is available in Malaysia through two main channels: retail, via TikTok Shop and Shopee for personal daily use, and wholesale, for cafes, retail stores, and distributors who want to stock it. Here's how to choose the right one, plus where else to look if you're near an Arabic grocery store in areas like Ampang or KL.

Buying Arabic Gum for personal use (retail)

If you want Arabic Gum powder for your own daily routine, retail is the fastest route. Sihatree sells directly to consumers across Malaysia through:

  • TikTok Shop — browse the full flavour range and check out directly in-app.
  • Shopee — same product range, with Shopee's standard delivery and buyer protection.
  • Berkat Madinah Store — the official supplier's own store, stocking the full range of flavoured Arabic Gum direct.

Both platforms ship nationwide, so whether you're in Kuala Lumpur, Ampang, Johor Bahru, Penang, or East Malaysia, delivery works the same way. See our flavour range — Original, Berry Blend, Mango, Pineapple, and Pomegranate — to pick what suits your taste.

Buying Arabic Gum in bulk (wholesale)

If you run a retail store, supermarket, café, or online shop and want to stock Arabic Gum powder, wholesale is the better fit. Sihatree works with resellers, distributors, and corporate buyers across Malaysia, and offers:

  • Bulk pricing scaled to order quantity
  • Marketing support materials for retail partners
  • Halal-certified stock, ready for shelf

Submit an inquiry through our wholesale form and our team typically responds within 1–2 business days with pricing and minimum order details.

What about Arabic grocery stores in KL or Ampang?

Ampang and parts of Kuala Lumpur have a well-established Middle Eastern community, and traditional Arabic Gum crystals — the raw, unprocessed form — can sometimes be found at Arabic or Middle Eastern grocery stores in these areas. That's a valid option if you want the raw crystal form for traditional preparation.

Sihatree's product is different: it's a milled, ready-to-mix powder format designed to dissolve instantly in water or drinks, with consistent quality control and JAKIM Halal certification behind every batch — see our Halal certification guide for details. If convenience and consistency matter more than sourcing raw crystals yourself, the powder format is the simpler choice.

Shipping and delivery across Malaysia

Both TikTok Shop and Shopee ship nationwide from local warehouses, so delivery times and costs follow each platform's standard rates rather than anything Sihatree-specific — typically a few business days within Peninsular Malaysia, with slightly longer timelines to Sabah and Sarawak. Wholesale orders are arranged separately once quantity and destination are confirmed through the inquiry form, and larger orders can be coordinated for direct delivery to a store or warehouse.

Buying within Klang Valley and Kuala Lumpur

For buyers in KL, Petaling Jaya, Ampang, Shah Alam, and the wider Klang Valley, delivery times through TikTok Shop and Shopee are typically on the faster end nationally, since most fulfilment centres serving these platforms sit within or close to the Klang Valley. If you're local to the area and want to skip the wait entirely, keep an eye on our official social channels for any pop-up or in-person availability announcements.

Common mistakes to avoid when buying

  • Buying from unofficial resellers — stick to our official TikTok Shop, Shopee, and Berkat Madinah Store storefronts to guarantee authentic stock and Halal-certified batches.
  • Assuming raw crystals and powder are interchangeable — if a recipe or routine calls for the milled powder format, raw crystals from a grocery store won't dissolve the same way.
  • Not checking the Halal certificate — always confirm certification directly rather than relying on packaging claims alone; see our Halal guide for how.

If you're stocking a store or café, the wholesale channel will almost always be more cost-effective than repeated retail orders.
