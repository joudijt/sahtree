Bring Sihatree to Your Shelves

We partner with retailers, cafes, distributors, and corporate buyers across Malaysia — with bulk pricing, marketing support, and a Halal-friendly product built for retail shelves.

Submit an Inquiry WhatsApp Us

Sihatree was built as a retail-first brand, not adapted into one after the fact. The packaging, pack size, and flavour range were all designed with a shelf and a checkout counter in mind, not just a factory carton — which is why bringing it into a new store or menu tends to be a straightforward conversation rather than a lengthy negotiation over formats and minimums.

Every flavour is naturally suitable for Halal consumers, so there's no separate approval process to manage across different SKUs. And because the range already sells through TikTok Shop and Shopee, wholesale partners aren't introducing an unknown product to their customers — in many cases, it's one they may already recognise from social media or word of mouth.

We also try to keep the conversation practical rather than sales-driven. Not every partner needs the same thing — a cafe adding one drink option to a menu has different needs from a distributor planning multi-state coverage — so the first conversation is mostly about understanding what would actually work for your business, not pushing a fixed package.
