Arabic Gum Gift Sets for Raya & Special Occasions in Malaysia

July 16, 2026 · 5 min read · By Sihatree

A practical, Halal-friendly gift that fits right into Raya visiting season.

Arabic gum makes a thoughtful Raya gift because it's practical, Halal-friendly, and fits naturally into Malaysia's open-house visiting culture. Here's why it works well as a gift, and simple ways to put a set together.

Why Arabic gum makes a good Raya gift

A good festive gift is something the recipient will actually use — Arabic gum checks that box in a few specific ways.

Practical and consumable

Unlike decorative gifts that may sit unused, a wellness drink powder gets used up as part of daily life, which many recipients appreciate more than another ornament or trinket.

Halal-friendly, safe for any Muslim recipient

Because it's Halal-friendly, it's a safe choice to give without needing to check dietary suitability first — see our Halal guide for the details.

Fits the Raya open-house routine

Raya open houses revolve around food and drink shared with visiting family and friends, which makes a flavoured drink powder a natural fit for the season rather than an unrelated novelty gift.

Gift set ideas

Single flavour pack

The simplest gift — one pack of the recipient's favourite or most popular flavour.

Flavour variety set

Two or three different flavour packs bundled together so they can compare and choose a favourite.

Paired with dates

Combine a pack with a box of dates for a gift that fits the festive food table.

Paired with tea

A pack alongside a good tea blend makes a simple, thoughtful hosting-season gift.

Wrapped as a hamper add-on

Add a pack into an existing Raya hamper or basket alongside other treats.

Bulk gifting for guests

Order in bulk to hand out to open-house guests, staff, or extended family.

Other special occasions it suits

Beyond Raya, the same reasoning applies to other gifting moments — Aidiladha, family gatherings, get-well wishes, or simply a thank-you gift for a host. If you're choosing a flavour for someone who hasn't tried it before, our beginner flavour guide is a good starting point.

Not sure which flavour to gift? Original is the safest all-round choice for someone whose taste you don't know well, while a fruit flavour like Mango tends to appeal to a wider range of palates at a festive gathering.

Buying for gifting

For a few gift sets, ordering retail packs individually works fine. For larger quantities — corporate gifting, staff hampers, or handing out to many open-house guests — see our wholesale ordering option for bulk pricing.
