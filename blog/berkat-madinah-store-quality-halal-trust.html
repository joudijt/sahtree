Quality, Halal Trust, and Community: What Stands Behind Berkat Madinah Store

July 20, 2026 · 5 min read · By Sihatree

Quality and credibility — the two values Berkat Madinah Store says everything else is built on.

Berkat Madinah Store names quality and credibility as its two core values, and points to a reported 5-star customer rating as the result. Beyond the mission statement, those values show up in three concrete places: how it sources, how consistent its branches are, and how it treats the community around it.

What do "quality" and "credibility" actually mean here?

Berkat Madinah Store states its mission is to provide "diverse Arab food of the highest quality," built on exactly two values: quality and credibility. Values on their own are just words — what makes them checkable is whether the sourcing and consistency behind them hold up, which is where the next two sections come in.

How does sourcing from 15+ countries support quality control?

Rather than each branch or product line sourcing independently, Berkat Madinah Store runs its import and distribution through a single wholesale line covering over 15 countries. That centralised sourcing is what lets the same dates, spices, and pantry staples show up consistently whether a customer is buying from a retail shelf, a wholesale order, or a café dish.

Does "quality" stop at the product, or extend to how the business treats people?

Berkat Madinah Store's stated practices go beyond the product itself — using recycled paper as part of its environmental efforts, and offering employment opportunities to people with special needs. Neither is framed as a marketing campaign; both are listed alongside the same quality-and-credibility values that describe the rest of the business, which is worth noting for buyers who weigh that kind of thing when choosing where to spend.

What does this mean for Sihatree buyers specifically?

Sihatree's Arabic Gum Powder is supplied by Berkat Madinah Store, so the same quality and sourcing standards described here sit behind every pack, alongside the product's own naturally halal-friendly ingredients — Sihatree does not currently hold a formal JAKIM Halal certificate.
