How to Mix Arabic Gum Powder: Recipes & Serving Ideas

July 16, 2026 · 6 min read · By Sihatree

Getting the stir right is the difference between a smooth drink and a clumpy one.

Mix Arabic gum powder by stirring it continuously into a full glass of water, juice, or another drink for about 20-30 seconds until fully dissolved. It works in both hot and cold liquids — here's the method that avoids clumping, plus five easy ways to serve it.

How do you mix Arabic gum powder?

The method is simple, but the order matters more than people expect: stirring continuously while the powder goes in dissolves it far more evenly than dumping it in and stirring afterward.

Cold water or juice method

Add the powder to a full glass of cold water, juice, or a cold drink of your choice, then stir continuously for 20-30 seconds. A full glass (roughly 250ml) gives the powder enough liquid to dissolve into evenly, rather than a small amount that clumps quickly.

Hot drinks method

Arabic gum powder also dissolves in hot drinks like tea or warm water, though the technique is slightly different from cold mixing.

Avoiding clumping

Let boiling liquid cool for a minute or two before adding the powder — extreme heat can cause the surface of the powder to clump before it has a chance to dissolve through. Stir the moment it's added, not after.

Water temperature tips

Warm (not boiling) water or tea works well and dissolves just as completely as cold water when stirred properly — temperature affects how quickly it dissolves, not whether it fully dissolves.

5 easy ways to drink Arabic gum powder

Original in plain water

The simplest option — mix into a glass of water for the ingredient in its purest form.

Berry Blend in sparkling water

Stir into chilled sparkling water for a refreshing, fizzy alternative to plain water.

Mango in a smoothie

Add during blending so it distributes evenly through fruit and yoghurt smoothies.

Pineapple in iced tea

Mix into chilled tea for a fruity twist on a everyday afternoon drink.

Pomegranate in warm water

A cosy option for evenings — stir into warm (not boiling) water after it cools slightly.

Can you cook or bake with Arabic gum powder?

Sihatree's powder is designed primarily as a drink additive, not a baking ingredient. It can be stirred into room-temperature or warm foods like yoghurt or overnight oats, but sustained high oven heat isn't the intended use case — stick to drinks and soft foods for the most consistent results.

Common mixing mistakes to avoid

  • Adding it to a very small amount of liquid — a full glass dissolves it far more evenly than a few sips' worth of water.
  • Adding it to boiling liquid — let it cool slightly first to avoid surface clumping.
  • Stirring only after adding — stir continuously while the powder goes in, not just afterward.
  • Confusing raw crystals with the milled powder — raw Arabic gum crystals don't dissolve the same way; Sihatree's format is milled specifically for this.

Best time of day to drink it

There's no single required time, but many people find mixing it 20-30 minutes before a meal or first thing in the morning works well as a routine. See our weight-management guide for why timing before meals can matter, and our dosage guide for how much to use per serving.
