Arabic Gum Price in Malaysia: What Should You Expect to Pay?

July 16, 2026 · 5 min read · By Sihatree

Price depends on more than the product alone — here's what actually shapes it.

Arabic gum pricing in Malaysia varies by pack size, flavour, and retailer promotions, so the live listing at Berkat Madinah Store is always more accurate than any fixed figure. Here's what actually shapes the price, and how to check current pricing safely.

What affects Arabic gum pricing?

A few consistent factors explain most of the difference in price you'll see between listings and platforms.

Pack size and quantity

Larger packs or multi-pack bundles typically offer a better per-gram price than a single small pack, similar to most packaged goods.

Flavour variant

Flavoured variants can carry a slightly different price from the plain Original due to added ingredients, though the difference is usually modest rather than dramatic.

Retail vs wholesale pricing

Retail (single pack) pricing

Retail pricing through platforms like TikTok Shop and Shopee is aimed at individual consumers buying one or a few packs at a time.

Wholesale/bulk pricing

Wholesale pricing is scaled to order quantity and intended for retailers, cafes, supermarkets, and distributors — see our buying guide for how the wholesale inquiry process works.

Why you won't find a fixed price listed everywhere
