Sourcing Arab Products for Your Business: Berkat Madinah's Wholesale Service

July 20, 2026 · 5 min read · By Sihatree

Wholesale sourcing at scale — the business line behind Berkat Madinah Store's retail shelves.

Berkat Madinah Store runs wholesale as one of its three core business lines, importing and distributing food products from over 15 countries — a real sourcing option for small retailers, cafes, and home-based sellers in Malaysia, not just large distributors. Here's how it actually works, and what to confirm before ordering.

What does Berkat Madinah Store's wholesale line actually cover?

Wholesale sits alongside retail branches and the company's restaurants and cafés as one of three stated business lines. In the company's own description, it means "importing and distributing food products from over 15 countries to meet market demands." In practice, that's the same sourcing pipeline behind the more than 6,000 products stocked across Berkat Madinah Store's retail shelves — dates, spices, nuts and seeds, canned foods, olive oil, and more.

Who is this actually useful for?

Wholesale buying is typically associated with large distributors, but the same sourcing line is genuinely useful at smaller scale too — a home-based seller putting together Raya hampers, a small café adding Arab pantry items to its menu, or a reseller on Shopee or TikTok Shop looking for stock. Berkat Madinah Store doesn't publish a separate "small business" programme beyond its general wholesale line, so the honest starting point is treating it as the same channel larger buyers use, then confirming terms directly for a smaller order.

How do you actually start a wholesale order?

Berkat Madinah Store can be reached directly by phone, WhatsApp, or email, or visited in person at its Ampang Cash & Carry branch, which is set up specifically for larger and bulk purchases rather than single-item retail shopping. Neither a minimum order size nor a published price list exists for wholesale, so the practical next step for a small or home-based buyer is to reach out directly and confirm terms before assuming anything.

What about wholesale through Sihatree specifically?

Sihatree's wholesale inquiry form is fulfilled by Berkat Madinah Store, so a retailer, café, or distributor sourcing flavoured Arabic Gum Powder goes through the same relationship covered in this article, not a separate process.
