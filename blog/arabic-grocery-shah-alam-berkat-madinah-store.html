Arabic Grocery in Shah Alam: Berkat Madinah Store Branch Guide

July 20, 2026 · 5 min read · By Sihatree

Dates by the basketful — a staple of the Shah Alam branch shelf.

Berkat Madinah Store runs one of its five Malaysian branches in Shah Alam, at Desa Alam — a full Arabic grocery stocking the same 6,000-plus product range as its other outlets, including Sihatree's flavoured Arabic Gum Powder. Here's what's on the shelf, and how delivery works if Shah Alam isn't convenient for you.

Where exactly is the Shah Alam branch?

The branch sits at No. 46, Jalan Pegaga C U12/C, Desa Alam, Seksyen U12, Shah Alam, Selangor — one of five Berkat Madinah Store outlets across the Klang Valley, alongside two branches in Ampang, plus Kajang and Gombak. It's a standard retail branch, geared toward everyday shopping rather than the bulk-focused Cash & Carry format used at the Ampang outlet.

What can you find on the shelves?

Every Berkat Madinah Store branch draws from the same wholesale sourcing pipeline covering more than 15 countries, so Shah Alam carries the same core range shoppers would find at any other outlet.

What if Shah Alam isn't near you?

Berkat Madinah Store's branches are concentrated in the Klang Valley, so not every buyer in Malaysia can reach one in person. The same products are available to order online through Shopee, Lazada, and TikTok Shop, with delivery reaching most areas of the country — the Shah Alam shelf and the online storefront carry the same range. Buyers can also browse directly at madinah.com.my.

How does this connect to Sihatree?

Sihatree's Arabic Gum Powder is supplied by Berkat Madinah Store, the same business behind the Shah Alam branch, so the product on this shelf is backed by the same sourcing and Halal certification covered in our supplier guide.
