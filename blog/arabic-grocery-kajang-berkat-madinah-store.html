Arabic Grocery in Kajang: Berkat Madinah Store Branch Guide

July 20, 2026 · 5 min read · By Sihatree

Nuts, dates, and pantry staples — the everyday range at the Kajang branch.

Berkat Madinah Store runs one of its five Malaysian branches in Kajang, at Pearl Avenue — an Arabic grocery serving both Kajang and neighbouring Bangi, stocking the same wide product range as its other outlets, including Sihatree's flavoured Arabic Gum Powder.

Where exactly is the Kajang branch?

The branch is on the ground floor of Pearl Avenue Shop, Jalan Pasir Emas, 43000 Kajang, Selangor — one of five Berkat Madinah Store outlets across the Klang Valley, alongside two branches in Ampang, plus Shah Alam and Gombak. Kajang sits right next to Bangi, so shoppers from either town can reach it without travelling deeper into the Klang Valley.

What can you find on the shelves?

Like every Berkat Madinah Store branch, Kajang draws from the same wholesale sourcing pipeline covering more than 15 countries — the same core range shoppers would find at Ampang, Shah Alam, or Gombak.

Dates

Multiple varieties, a staple of the shelf year-round.

Spices & Herbs

Whole and ground spices for Middle Eastern cooking.

Nuts & Dried Fruit

Snacking and cooking varieties, loose or packaged.

Canned & Pantry Goods

Everyday staples alongside the specialty range.

Tea & Coffee

Traditional blends stocked alongside everyday brands.

What if you're further from Kajang or Bangi?

Berkat Madinah Store's branches are concentrated in the Klang Valley, so buyers elsewhere in Malaysia can order the same products online through Shopee, Lazada, and TikTok Shop, with delivery reaching most areas of the country. Buyers can also browse directly at madinah.com.my.

How does this connect to Sihatree?

Sihatree's Arabic Gum Powder is supplied by Berkat Madinah Store, the same business behind the Kajang branch, so the product on this shelf is backed by the same sourcing and Halal compliance covered in our supplier guide. For the branch where the company's story began, see our Ampang branch guide.
