How to Verify Your Arabic Gum Is Genuine (Not Counterfeit)

July 16, 2026 · 6 min read · By Sihatree

Checking your pack the moment it arrives is the easiest way to catch a problem early.

Genuine Arabic gum should arrive in sealed, undamaged packaging with a matching batch code and clear contact details — bought through a recognised retail channel. Here's a practical checklist, why counterfeit stock is a real risk in Malaysia, and what to do if something looks off.

Why counterfeit or unofficial stock is a real risk

Arabic gum's popularity means unofficial resellers sometimes sell repackaged, adulterated, or simply mislabelled products, often at a lower price with no traceable supply chain. Buying from an unverified source means there's no way to confirm what's actually in the pack, or whether the Halal claim is real.

Signs of a genuine pack

Sealed packaging

No signs of tampering, resealing, or damage to the original seal.

Correct branding & label

Matches Sihatree's official packaging design — no cert number to check, since Sihatree doesn't hold a JAKIM certificate.

Batch or lot code

Traceable to a specific production run, not just a generic label.

Authorised store purchase

Bought directly through the brand's own retailer, not a reseller.

Consistent texture & colour

Matches what's expected for that flavour, batch to batch.

Contact info on pack

A real, reachable contact — not a blank or missing label.

What to do if you suspect a counterfeit

Don't self-diagnose

Stop using the product, keep the packaging and proof of purchase, and contact the seller or supplier directly. Don't continue using a product you suspect may be counterfeit or adulterated.

The safest step isn't inspecting the pack after the fact — it's buying from a recognised source in the first place.
