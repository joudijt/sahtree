Gut Health

Prebiotic-Style Fibre Support

Arabic Gum is a soluble fibre known for its prebiotic-style properties. It can feed the good bacteria already living in your gut.

It isn't a probiotic itself — that would mean live bacteria. Instead, it's a fibre that supports a healthy gut environment as part of a balanced diet.

The terms get mixed up a lot, so it helps to be precise. A probiotic adds live organisms to your gut.

A prebiotic — which is what Arabic Gum is — feeds the organisms already there. Both can be useful, but they solve different problems, and Sihatree is specifically the second kind.

Who This Is For

Sihatree tends to suit people already looking for a small, sustainable change rather than a dramatic one — someone who wants to add more fibre into an otherwise normal diet without overhauling it, or who has tried other fibre supplements before and found them unpleasant to take consistently. It's also a common starting point for people newly exploring gut-health habits who want something low-effort to begin with, rather than a complicated multi-step regimen. It also suits a range of ages — younger adults building a routine, busy parents, and older adults who prefer something simple to keep up with day to day.

It isn't a replacement for a balanced diet, and it isn't marketed as a treatment for any condition — it's a daily-use fibre ingredient, and the honest way to think about it is as one small, consistent input rather than a single fix. Anyone with an existing health condition, or who is pregnant or breastfeeding, should check with a doctor before adding any new fibre supplement to their routine.
