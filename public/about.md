# About — Sihatree

Sihatree Arabic Gum Powder is a natural water-soluble prebiotic dietary fibre made from the dried purified sap of the Acacia tree (Acacia senegal / Acacia seyal), milled into an instant powder and sold in Malaysia in five flavours — Original, Berry Blend, Mango, Pineapple and Pomegranate — in a 150 g resealable pouch. It is sold in Malaysia only through Berkat Madinah Store, an Arabic grocery founded in 2010 that runs five Klang Valley branches, carries 6,000+ products and imports from 15+ countries.
