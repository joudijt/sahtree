Arabic Grocery in Gombak: Berkat Madinah Store Branch Guide

August 25, 2026 · 5 min read · By Sihatree

Shelves of imported pantry goods — illustrative of the kind of stock a Berkat Madinah Store branch carries.

Berkat Madinah Store's fourth real Klang Valley branch sits in Gombak, at KL Traders Square, trading locally as Arabian Village. It's a full Arabic grocery stocking the same wide range as the Ampang, Kajang, and Shah Alam branches, including Sihatree's flavoured Arabic Gum Powder — here's exactly where it is, what's on the shelf, and how delivery covers the rest of Malaysia.

Where exactly is the Gombak branch?

The branch is at 289, Jalan Gombak No 95-G, (Block E), KL Traders Square, 53100 Kuala Lumpur. Locally it also trades as Arabian Village — the same name used on Berkat Madinah Store's Shopee storefront — so shoppers searching for either name land at the same address. It's one of four confirmed Berkat Madinah Store branches across the Klang Valley, alongside two outlets in Ampang (including the bulk-focused Cash & Carry format), Kajang, and Shah Alam. KL Traders Square is a commercial block along Jalan Gombak, so the branch sits among other retail units rather than as a standalone shopfront — worth keeping in mind if you're navigating there for the first time.

What can you find on the shelves?

Every Berkat Madinah Store branch draws from the same wholesale sourcing pipeline covering more than 15 countries, so Gombak carries the same core range shoppers would find at any other outlet. That consistency is the point of a shared sourcing pipeline across four branches — the shelf doesn't get thinner just because a branch is smaller or newer than the Ampang head office.

What are Gombak branch's opening hours?

Berkat Madinah Store doesn't publish branch opening hours online, and Gombak is no exception. Rather than guess, the reliable way to confirm hours before making a trip is a quick message on WhatsApp, or checking the branch listing directly on madinah.com.my. The same applies if you want to confirm stock of a specific Sihatree flavour before travelling.

What if Gombak isn't near you?

Berkat Madinah Store's branches are concentrated in the Klang Valley, so not every buyer in Malaysia can reach one in person. The same products are available to order online through TikTok Shop and Shopee (Arabian Village Malaysia), with delivery reaching most areas of the country — the Gombak shelf and the online storefront carry the same range. Buyers can also browse directly at madinah.com.my.

How does this connect to Sihatree?

Sihatree's Arabic Gum Powder is supplied by Berkat Madinah Store, the same business behind the Gombak branch, so the product on this shelf is backed by the same sourcing and Halal guidelines covered in our supplier guide. For the branch where the company's story began, see our Ampang branch guide.

Whether you shop at the Gombak shelf, order through the app, or message on WhatsApp first, you're buying from the same supplier every time — there's no separate "Sihatree store" and "Berkat Madinah Store" to compare, and no other Arabic grocery carries this product.

Four branches, one sourcing pipeline — Gombak carries the same range as every other Berkat Madinah Store outlet.
